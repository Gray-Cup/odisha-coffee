"Specialty" has a technical meaning: green coffee that scores 80+ on the SCA 100-point scale, with near-zero defects, full traceability, and careful processing. In practice it means you can name the farm, the variety, the process and the crop year — and the coffee is good enough that those details matter.

## What makes a coffee specialty

- **Traceability** — farm or washing-station level, not just "India".
- **Processing** — deliberate washed / natural / honey / anaerobic, done with control.
- **Elevation & variety** — stated, because they shape the cup.
- **Micro-lots** — small separations kept apart instead of bulked.
- **Freshness** — current crop, recent roast.

## Odisha as an emerging specialty origin

Koraput ticks these boxes precisely because it is new: small farms, named partners, processing variety, and lots still being separated and experimented with rather than blended into commodity flow.
