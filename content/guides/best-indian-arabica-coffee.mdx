## Indian Arabica varieties

- **S795**: the Indian workhorse; rust-tolerant, balanced, nutty-chocolatey.
- **Chandragiri**: sweeter, fuller body, good disease resistance.
- **Cauvery (Catimor)**: higher yield, cleaner acidity.
- **HSD (Hibrido de Timor)**: clean, citric, "washed-Ethiopia" character; increasingly kept as specialty micro-lots in Koraput.
- **SLN 9**: an Ethiopian-Sudan cross grown in a few Koraput lots for aromatics.

## What altitude and processing add

Higher-grown Arabica (1,000 m+) is denser and brighter. Washed processing keeps it clean and acidic; natural adds body and dark fruit; honey adds sweetness.
