## The two species

| | Arabica | Robusta |
|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | ~1.2% | ~2.2% |
| Taste | sweeter, acidic, aromatic | bitter, woody, grainy |
| Body | lighter–medium | heavy |
| Crema | thin, fades | thick, lasting |
| Grows at | 900–1,800 m | 200–900 m |

## What the ratio does to filter coffee

- **95/5, 90/10**: almost pure Arabica: aromatic, smooth, gentle. Best if you drink your kaapi lighter or with less milk.
- **80/20**: a touch more strength and crema, still Arabica-forward.
- **70/30**: the classic strong South Indian cup: bold, bitter-sweet, thick crema, more caffeine. Stands up to lots of hot milk and sugar.

More Robusta = more body, more caffeine, more bitterness, more crema, lower cost.
