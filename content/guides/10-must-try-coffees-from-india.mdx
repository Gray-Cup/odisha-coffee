## Odisha — Koraput, Eastern Ghats

India's youngest specialty origin. Smallholder Arabica (S795, Chandragiri, HSD) grown 900–1,200 m, processed washed, natural and honey. Washed lots: cocoa, brown sugar, mild citrus. Naturals: dark cherry, chocolate.

## Northeast — Assam & Arunachal Pradesh

Low-altitude, monsoon-heavy, mostly newer plantings. Softer acidity, nutty and mellow, often used to add smoothness and body to blends. Worth trying for how different it is from hill-grown coffee.

## South — Karnataka (Chikmagalur, Coorg, Baba Budan)

The established belt: large estates, shade-grown Arabica and Robusta, monsooned Malabar as a regional oddity. Balanced, full-bodied, low-acid, spice and chocolate notes.

## The comparison that teaches you most

Brew a **washed Koraput Arabica** next to a **Karnataka estate washed** and a **Northeast lot**, all as filter or pour-over. Altitude, soil and processing differences become obvious in one sitting.
