## What natural processing does

The whole coffee cherry is dried intact — on raised beds or patios — for 3–5 weeks. The bean ferments slowly inside the fruit, absorbing sugars and fruity, sometimes fermented, compounds before hulling.

## The cup

Heavy body, low acidity, dark fruit (cherry, berry, raisin), chocolate, sometimes a boozy or wine-like edge. Big, sweet and rich rather than clean and bright.

## Roasting considerations

- **Uneven density and sugar** — easy to scorch. Ease off gas through the middle.
- **Longer development** (DTR 22–25%) to integrate the fruit.
- **Watch first crack closely** — naturals can run away.
- **QC every batch** — drying variation means more cup inconsistency and the odd ferment fault.
