Koraput is not one flavour. Within the district, altitude runs from roughly 800 m in the valleys to 1,300 m on the ridges, farms range from certified-organic smallholdings to foundation-run estates, and lots are processed washed, natural, honey and anaerobic. All of that shows up in the cup.

## What changes the Koraput cup

- **Altitude**: higher lots are denser, brighter, more acidic.
- **Variety**: S795 is balanced and nutty; Chandragiri is sweeter-bodied; HSD (Hibrido de Timor) is clean and citric.
- **Processing**: washed = clean and cocoa-ish; natural = dark fruit; honey = sweet middle ground.
- **Farm**: organic vs conventional, shade density, and drying discipline all matter.
