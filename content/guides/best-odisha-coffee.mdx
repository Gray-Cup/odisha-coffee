## Why Odisha coffee tastes different

The Eastern Ghats are lower and drier than the Western Ghats estates of Karnataka, with a distinct monsoon pattern and mostly tribal smallholder farming rather than large estates. The washed lots tend to be clean and mild with cocoa and citrus; the naturals lean into dark fruit and chocolate. Because the farms are small and lots are kept separate, there is real variation from one to the next.

## How to try Odisha coffee properly

Buy one **washed** and one **natural** Koraput lot, brew both as filter or pour-over over a few days, and you will quickly hear what the region sounds like. From there, honey and anaerobic lots are the interesting next step.
