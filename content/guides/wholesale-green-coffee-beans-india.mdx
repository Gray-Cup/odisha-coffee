## Wholesale vs retail green coffee

Retail green coffee is sold by the kilo at a flat rate. Wholesale is sold by the **bag or lot** with **tiered pricing** (the ₹/kg rate drops as volume rises) and usually a minimum order quantity per lot.

## What to lock down

- **Grade & screen size**: pick for even roasting at your batch size.
- **Moisture (10–12%) and crop year**: in writing.
- **Consistency commitment**: can the supplier hold the same lot profile across repeat orders this season?
- **Price tiers & MOQ**: get the full schedule.
- **Documentation**: FSSAI, lot traceability, and for export: phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin, ICO marks.
- **Packaging**: GrainPro / vacuum liners in jute for shelf life.

## Logistics

Domestic freight arrives in days. Export is sea freight, 4–8 weeks, plan inventory around it. Request a **pre-shipment sample** and approve it before the lot ships.

## Sourcing model

Buying against named Koraput partner farms gives farm-level traceability for your menu or packaging without running your own import chain. Enquire for the current bulk price schedule and available lots.
