## How Indian coffee varies

- **Odisha (Koraput)** — Eastern Ghats, smallholder Arabica, 900–1,200 m. Clean and cocoa-ish washed, dark-fruited natural.
- **Karnataka** — Western Ghats estates, Arabica and Robusta, full-bodied, low-acid, chocolate and spice.
- **Northeast (Assam, Arunachal)** — low-grown, mellow, nutty, soft.
- **Kerala / Tamil Nadu** — mostly Robusta and traditional filter blends.
