## Practical protocol

- **Dose:** roughly 3 mg per kg bodyweight, for most people that's 1–2 cups (150–300 mg).
- **Timing:** 45–60 minutes before training.
- **Black:** skip milk and sugar pre-workout if you want to train fasted or avoid GI discomfort.
- **Ceiling:** keep total daily caffeine under ~400 mg; more doesn't help and hurts sleep.

## Roast and bean

A **medium roast Arabica** gives a clean, easy-to-drink black cup with predictable caffeine. Robusta-heavy blends hit harder on caffeine if that's your goal, at the cost of taste.
