## Single-origin

Coffee from one place and one crop. Tastes of that origin and processing.

**Buy it when:** you want to explore terroir, you brew filter or pour-over, you like variety season to season, you want traceability.

**Trade-off:** it changes, this year's lot won't taste identical to last year's.

## Blends

Two or more coffees combined for a target cup.

**Buy it when:** you want the same taste every day, you pull milk-based espresso (blends give reliable body and crema), or you're making South Indian filter coffee (a blend by tradition).

**Trade-off:** individual origin character is smoothed out by design.

## It's not a quality ranking

A great blend is a deliberate composition; a great single-origin is a great lot shown honestly. Neither is "more serious".
