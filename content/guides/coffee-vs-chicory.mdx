## What each is

- **Coffee**: the roasted seed of the coffee fruit. Contains caffeine and hundreds of aromatic compounds.
- **Chicory**: the roasted, ground taproot of the chicory plant (a relative of the dandelion). No caffeine, no coffee compounds.

## How they compare

| | Coffee | Chicory |
|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | yes | none |
| Taste | acidic, aromatic, complex | woody, bitter-sweet, earthy |
| Body | varies by process | thick, syrupy |
| Cost | higher | lower |
| In the cup | the flavour | body, colour, "stretch" |

## Why Indian filter coffee uses chicory

It started as a cost measure, chicory is cheaper and stretches the coffee. Over generations the taste became the *expected* flavour of South Indian filter coffee, especially in Tamil Nadu. A common blend is 70% coffee / 30% chicory.

## Which should you buy?

Want caffeine and coffee flavour only? **Pure Arabica, no chicory.** Want the traditional South Indian cup? A **coffee/chicory blend**.
