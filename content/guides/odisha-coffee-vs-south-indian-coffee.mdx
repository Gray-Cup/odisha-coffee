## Geography and farming

| | Odisha (Koraput) | South India (Karnataka) |
|---|---|---|
| Range | Eastern Ghats | Western Ghats |
| Altitude | ~900–1,200 m | ~900–1,600 m |
| Farms | tribal smallholders, new milling | large established estates |
| Rain | shorter, sharper monsoon | long, heavy monsoon |
| Age as origin | ~2 decades of serious growth | 150+ years |

## Cup character

- **Koraput washed**: clean, medium body, cocoa, brown sugar, mild citrus.
- **Koraput natural**: dark cherry, chocolate, heavier.
- **Karnataka estate**: full body, low acidity, chocolate, spice, sometimes a heavier "monsooned" style.

Koraput lots vary more bag to bag (small farms, new processing); Karnataka estates deliver more consistency at scale.
