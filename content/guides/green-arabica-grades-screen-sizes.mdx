## Screen size

Screen size is bean width measured in **1/64 inch**. A "screen 17" bean passes a screen 18 sieve but not a 17.

| Screen | Rough size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 17–19 | large (AAA/A) | most uniform, roasts evenest |
| 15–16 | medium (AA/A) | specialty workhorse |
| 13–14 | small (B/BBB) | roasts faster, less evenly |
| PB | round | peaberry, sized separately |

Uniformity matters more than absolute size, a tight screen range roasts predictably.

## Moisture

**10–12%** is the target. Too dry (under 9%) and beans bake and taste flat; too wet (over 12.5%) and they mould and fade in storage. Ask for the reading.

## Density

Higher-grown beans are denser and need more heat / a steeper roast curve. Density is often implied by altitude and grade but can be measured directly.

## Processing (on top of size)

Screen size and grade describe the *bean*; washed / natural / honey / anaerobic describe the *flavour*. Two AAA lots can taste completely different.

## Reading a spec sheet

A good lot sheet lists: grade, screen size, moisture %, crop year, process, altitude, defect count, and a cupping note. If any of those is missing, ask before you buy.
