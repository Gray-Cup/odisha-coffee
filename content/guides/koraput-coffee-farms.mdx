## Koraput isn't one anonymous origin

Odisha coffee comes from a network of **partner farms and smallholder groups** across Koraput district, each with its own altitude, shade, varieties and drying setup. Treating them as one bag loses what makes the origin interesting.

## Some of the farms

- **Dream Hill**: higher-elevation Koraput lot known for clean, sweet honey-processed Arabica.
- **Brown Valley**: HSD and honey lots; also the source of a rare wild-foraged civet coffee.
- **Oak Winds**: washed Arabica, and experimental lots (SLN 9, natural strawberry-profile).
- **Koraput Organic**: certified-organic natural-process Arabica.
- **Panigrahi Foundation, Maa Mangala, Annapurna**: smallholder-federation lots, washed and honey S795.

The [full partner farm directory](/farms) lists elevations, varieties and processing per farm.

## Why farm-level sourcing matters

- **Traceability**: you know exactly where the coffee grew.
- **Accountability**: quality feedback reaches the people who can act on it.
- **Story**: for your café menu or bag, a named farm beats "Indian Arabica".
