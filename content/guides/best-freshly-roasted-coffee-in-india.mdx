## Why roast freshness matters

Roasted coffee releases CO₂ and volatile aromatics for weeks. The sweet spot is **4–30 days off roast** for filter and pour-over. Past ~6 weeks the aroma flattens, the sweetness drops, and the cup tastes papery or dull. Ground coffee stales in *days*, not weeks.

## How to check

- Look for a **roast date** (not just "best before"). No roast date is a red flag.
- Buy **whole bean**, grind at brew time.
- Buy a **quantity you'll finish in a month**.
- Bags should have a **one-way degassing valve**.
