## Where caffeine comes from

Caffeine is the coffee plant's natural pesticide. **Robusta** (Coffea canephora) has about **2.2% caffeine** by weight; **Arabica** about **1.2%**. So the single biggest lever on a strong cup is the Arabica:Robusta ratio.

Roast level is a myth here, light and dark roasts have almost the same caffeine per gram. Dark roast tastes stronger; it isn't.

## How to maximise the dose

1. **Robusta-forward blend** (70/30 or more Robusta).
2. **Higher dose per cup**, brew at 1:14 instead of 1:17.
3. **Big-volume method**, filter or French press mug over a single espresso.
4. **Two moderate cups** spaced out beats one huge one for sustained alertness.

## Don't overdo it

400 mg/day (~4 cups) is the general safe ceiling. More causes jitters, poor sleep and dependence without extra benefit.
