## How it works

1. **Decoction** — fine coffee powder in a two-chamber metal filter; hot water drips through slowly to make a concentrated brew.
2. **Milk** — boiling hot, added to the decoction.
3. **Sugar** — to taste.
4. **Pull** — poured back and forth between tumbler and davara to froth and cool it.

"Degree coffee" refers to decoction made with the first, strongest drip.

## The blend

Traditional kaapi is an **Arabica/Robusta blend**, often with chicory. Robusta gives body and crema; chicory adds a dark woody sweetness and stretches the coffee. Ratios from 90/10 (mild) to 70/30 (strong).

## Roast and grind

Medium-dark roast, fine grind. Freshness matters — buy small.
