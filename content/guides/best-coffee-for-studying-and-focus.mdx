## A study-session approach

- **Smaller cups, spaced out**: one moderate cup every 2–3 hours beats a triple-shot upfront.
- **Start ~30 minutes before** you need to focus.
- **Stop by early afternoon**: caffeine's ~5-hour half-life will wreck your sleep, and poor sleep destroys next-day learning.
- **Water alongside**: mild dehydration feels like brain fog.
- **Pair with a small snack** if studying long, to avoid a blood-sugar dip that caffeine masks.
