## Timing

Caffeine peaks in the blood **30–60 minutes** after drinking. Have your coffee about **45 minutes before** you start.

## Dose

Around **3 mg per kg bodyweight** is the researched performance dose, roughly 1–2 cups for most people. More doesn't add benefit and raises the chance of jitters, racing heart and GI upset.

## Tolerance

Regular coffee drinkers get less of an acute boost. Some people cycle caffeine (lower intake for 1–2 weeks) before an event to restore sensitivity. Not essential for everyday training.

## Black, usually

Black coffee before training avoids the dairy/sugar load if you train fasted or get stomach discomfort. If you tolerate milk fine, it barely matters.
