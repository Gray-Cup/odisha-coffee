## Coorg

- Western Ghats, ~750–1,100 m, high rainfall.
- Big Robusta production alongside Arabica, India's Robusta heartland.
- Estate-scale, shade-grown, often intercropped with pepper and cardamom.
- Cup: heavy body, low acidity, chocolate, nuts, mellow. Robusta lots are strong and earthy.

## Koraput

- Eastern Ghats, ~900–1,200 m, mostly Arabica.
- Smallholder, newer milling, more processing experimentation.
- Cup: cleaner, medium body, cocoa and citrus (washed), dark fruit (natural).

## Which should you try?

Want low-acid, full-bodied, milk-and-sugar-friendly coffee, or Robusta for a strong filter? Coorg. Want a cleaner, brighter, more single-origin-style Arabica? Koraput.
