## What chicory is

Chicory is the roasted, ground root of the chicory plant. It contains **no caffeine and no coffee**. Ground into filter coffee (commonly 10–30%), it adds body, a dark woody-sweet note, colour, and it stretches the coffee — historically a cost measure that became a taste people grew to love in South India.

## Why skip it

- You want **only coffee flavour** and aroma.
- You are sensitive to chicory's laxative/inulin effect in quantity.
- You are brewing pour-over or espresso where chicory muddies the cup.
