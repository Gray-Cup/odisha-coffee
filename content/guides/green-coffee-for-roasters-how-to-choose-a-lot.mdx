## The checklist

**1. Purpose** — What product is this for? Single-origin filter → washed, high-grown, clean. Espresso blend base → body and sweetness, natural or honey. House blend / volume → consistent commercial grade.

**2. Processing** — Sets flavour more than grade does. Match it to the product above.

**3. Moisture** — 10–12%. Drier lots roast faster and flatter; wetter lots risk mould and baking.

**4. Density / altitude** — Denser beans need more energy and a steeper curve. Affects your charge temperature and batch size.

**5. Screen size & uniformity** — Tight range = even development. Wide range = scorched and underdeveloped beans in the same batch.

**6. Defect count** — Higher grades cap primary/secondary defects. Hand-sort a sample and count.

**7. Crop year** — Current crop roasts predictably; past-crop goes papery and flat.

**8. Cup it** — Sample-roast 250 g, rest 24–48 h, cup against your standards. Nothing on the spec sheet replaces this.

**9. Consistency** — Can the supplier hold this lot's profile across repeat orders this season? Ask directly.
