## What to look for

- **Arabica**: not Robusta-heavy blends.
- **Light to medium roast**: dark roasts turn bitter and ashy black.
- **Washed or honey** for clean sweetness; **naturals** if you like fruit that reads as sweet.
- **Fresh roast, correct grind, right ratio** (1:16–1:17 for filter). Most "bitter black coffee" is actually stale or over-extracted coffee.

## Brew tips for black

- Filter or pour-over at 92–94°C, medium grind.
- French press for more body, 4 minutes, coarse grind.
- Taste as it cools, good black coffee gets sweeter, bad coffee gets worse.
