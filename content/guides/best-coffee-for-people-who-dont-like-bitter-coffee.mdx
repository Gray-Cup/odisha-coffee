## Where bitterness comes from

1. **Dark roast**, the darker the roast, the more carbon and bitter compounds. Switch to light or medium.
2. **Robusta**, much more bitter than Arabica. Choose Arabica or a low-Robusta blend.
3. **Chicory**, adds a woody bitterness to traditional filter blends. Try no-chicory.
4. **Over-extraction**, grind too fine, water too hot, brew too long. Coarsen the grind, use 92–94°C, keep filter contact under 4 minutes.
5. **Stale coffee**, old beans taste flat and harsh. Buy fresh, small.

## Brew for sweetness

- Grind medium-coarse.
- 1:16–1:17 coffee-to-water.
- 92–94°C, not boiling.
- Taste as it cools, sweetness comes forward.
