## The journey

**1. Farm**: Smallholders across Koraput (Pottangi, Semiliguda, Dasmantpur) grow Arabica under forest shade, often with pepper and banana.

**2. Harvest**: Ripe red cherries hand-picked, Nov–Feb.

**3. Processing**: Washed (pulped, fermented, washed), natural (dried whole), or honey (dried with mucilage).

**4. Drying**: To 10–12% moisture on raised beds, 14–30 days depending on method.

**5. Resting & hulling**: Rested in parchment, then hulled to the green bean.

**6. Grading**: Sorted by screen size and defects into A, AA, AAA, PB.

**7. Roasting**: 8–14 minutes; the roaster decides light, medium or dark.

**8. Brew**: Filter, pour-over, press or espresso.
