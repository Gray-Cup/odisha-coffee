## Powder vs whole beans

Whole beans, ground fresh, always taste better and stay fresh for weeks. Pre-ground powder is convenient but stales within days of opening. If you buy powder: buy small, buy often, keep it sealed and cool.

## Grind size

South Indian filter wants a **fine grind** — finer than drip, coarser than espresso. Too coarse and the decoction is weak and sour; too fine and it clogs the filter and turns bitter.

## Ratio and chicory

- **80/20, 90/10** Arabica/Robusta — smoother, aromatic.
- **70/30** — the strong classic.
- **With chicory** — traditional body and woody sweetness; **without** — pure coffee.
