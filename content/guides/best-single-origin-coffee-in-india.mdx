Single-origin means the coffee comes from **one place** (one region, estate or farm) and one crop. It tastes of that place. A blend mixes origins for a consistent, engineered cup.

## When to buy single-origin

- You want to taste terroir and processing clearly.
- You brew filter, pour-over or as a light espresso.
- You like variety season to season.

## When a blend is the right call

- You want the same cup every day.
- You pull milk-based espresso and want body + crema.
- Traditional South Indian filter, a blend by definition.

## India's single-origin regions

**Koraput, Odisha**: smallholder Arabica, Eastern Ghats, washed/natural/honey. Cocoa and citrus (washed), dark fruit (natural).
