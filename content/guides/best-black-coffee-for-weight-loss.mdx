Let's be straight: **black coffee does not cause meaningful fat loss on its own.** What it does is contribute near-zero calories (about 2 kcal a cup) versus 80–150 kcal for a sugary milk coffee, mildly raise metabolic rate for a few hours, and blunt appetite for some people. Swapping a daily 150 kcal latte for black coffee is a real calorie saving. The coffee is not a fat burner.

## What to look for

If you are switching to black coffee, the barrier is usually bitterness. Choose:

- **Arabica over Robusta** (less harsh, less caffeine).
- **A lighter or medium roast**: dark roasts taste more bitter and ashy black.
- **Washed or honey processing** for clean sweetness; **naturals** for fruit that reads as sweetness.
- **Fresh roast, proper grind**: stale or over-extracted coffee is what makes black coffee unpleasant.

## Timing and caffeine

Caffeine works within 30–45 minutes and lasts hours. For a workout, black coffee 30–45 minutes before training is well supported. Keep total daily caffeine under ~400 mg (roughly 4 cups) and avoid it within ~8 hours of sleep.
