## The morning problem

You want caffeine in your system before training but you don't have 20 minutes for a pour-over at 5:30 am.

## Fast options

- **Moka pot / South Indian filter**: start it while you change, ~4 minutes.
- **French press**: coarse grind, 4-minute steep, plunge, go.
- **Pre-ground filter coffee** kept fresh in the freezer, acceptable trade-off for speed.
- **AeroPress**: 90 seconds if you're organised.

Drink it 30–45 minutes before your session. If your workout is *immediately* on waking, drink during your warm-up, it'll kick in mid-session.
