## Sourcing routes

1. **Direct from origin / partner farms** — best traceability and price, needs volume and QC capacity.
2. **Green coffee importers / domestic suppliers** — smaller quantities, curated lots, faster.
3. **Auctions / curing works** — commodity grades, large volume.

For Koraput coffee, buying against named partner farms gives farm-level traceability without needing to run your own export chain.

## What to ask for

- **Sample** (100–250 g) before any volume order.
- **Spec sheet**: grade, screen size, moisture %, crop year, process, defect count.
- **Documentation**: FSSAI, lot/traceability reference, phytosanitary certificate for export.
- **MOQ and pricing tiers** — per-kg rate usually drops with quantity.
