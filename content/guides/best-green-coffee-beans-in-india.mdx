## How to shortlist

1. Match **process to product** (washed → filter, natural → espresso blend).
2. Match **grade to roaster** (uniform screen for even roasting at volume).
3. **Sample first**, roast 250 g and cup before ordering pallets.
4. Confirm **moisture (10–12%)** and **crop year**.
