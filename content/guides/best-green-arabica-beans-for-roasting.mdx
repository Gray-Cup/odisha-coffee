## Match the lot to the roast

**Light / filter roasts**: want clean, dense, high-grown washed lots. Acidity and origin clarity survive a light roast; defects don't hide.

## What to check before buying for a profile

- **Density & altitude**: sets your charge temp and airflow.
- **Moisture**: 10–12%; drier lots move faster through the roast.
- **Screen uniformity**: tight range = even development.
- **Crop year**: current crop for predictable behaviour.

## Always sample-roast first

Roast 250 g on your machine, cup at 24–48 hours, and only then commit to volume. A lot that reads perfect on paper can still roast unevenly on your specific machine.
