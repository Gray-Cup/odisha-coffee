## First decision: roasted or green?

- **Roasted** — ready to grind and brew. Buy this unless you own a roaster.
- **Green** — unroasted beans for home or commercial roasting. Sold by grade and lot.

## Second: which processing?

- **Washed** — clean, cocoa, citrus. Safest first buy.
- **Natural** — dark fruit, chocolate, heavier.
- **Honey** — sweet middle ground.

## Third: whole bean or ground?

Whole bean, always, if you have a grinder — grind at brew time. Only buy ground if you don't, and then buy small and use it within two weeks.

## Fourth: pack size

Start with **250 g**. Move to 500 g or 1 kg once you know you like the lot.
