## What actually raises the caffeine dose

- **More Robusta** in the blend — roughly double the caffeine of Arabica.
- **More coffee per cup** — a stronger brew ratio (1:14 vs 1:17).
- **Brew method** — a large filter/French press mug delivers more total caffeine than a single espresso, even though espresso tastes stronger.
- **Roast level barely matters** — light and dark roast have nearly identical caffeine by weight.

## Sensible limits

Caffeine peaks at 30–60 minutes and has a ~5-hour half-life. Keep under ~400 mg/day and avoid it within 8 hours of bed.
